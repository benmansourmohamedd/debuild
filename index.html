research

I am interested in how social, mathematical, environmental, and political systems become spatial.
01
Public space + social infrastructure
Third places, gathering, civic interiors, public institutions, access, and everyday forms of collective life.
02
Computation + algorithms
Computational visualization, modeling, simulation, algorithmic systems, and their use as architectural research methods.
03
Ethnography + anthropology
Observation, lived environments, cultural practice, informal spatial systems, and relationships between people and place.
04
Ethnomathematics
Cultural mathematics, vernacular geometry, alternative systems of measurement, pattern, proportion, and spatial reasoning.
05
Environmental systems
Climate, heat, water, material systems, infrastructure, technology, and the environmental conditions of everyday space.
06
Architecture + governance
Spatial policy, institutions, public systems, planning, and the ways governance becomes material through architecture.
