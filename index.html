I am a designer and researcher working across architecture, computation, ethnomathematics, and social inquiry.

My research examines how governance, cultural knowledge, and technical systems become spatial rules—and how those rules can be documented, modeled, and challenged.

01

Selected research

001

Algorithmic Thresholds of Access

A study of how eligibility, waiting, identification, and intake procedures become architectural systems that distribute access unevenly.

Washington, DC · 2025–ongoing · fieldwork and mapping
002

The Corridor Is an Algorithm

Comparative fieldwork on institutional interiors, documenting routing, sightlines, checkpoints, and behavioral scripts as architectures of compliance.

Baltimore, Maryland · 2024–2025 · comparative casework
003

Ethnomathematics of Repair

An archive of repair practices that reads measurement, fit, tolerance, sequence, and improvisation as situated mathematical knowledge.

DC–Baltimore region · 2024–ongoing · archive and protocol
004

deBUILD Research Archive

A computational publishing system for comparable socio-spatial casework, combining observations, analysis, diagrams, images, and references.

Digital · 2023–ongoing · editorial and system design
