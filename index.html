research
Ongoing questions, methods, and fields of inquiry.

I am interested in architecture not only as an object of design, but as a way of reading relationships between people, systems, knowledge, and space.

Computation
Algorithms, computational visualization, modeling, simulation, generative systems, and spatial computation as methods of design and inquiry.

Ethnomathematics
Cultural systems of measurement, pattern, spatial reasoning, construction, and mathematical knowledge.

Anthropology + Ethnography
Everyday occupation, cultural practice, lived environments, informal knowledge, and relationships between people and place.

Sociology
Institutions, governance, social organization, infrastructure, and the systems through which cities distribute resources, access, and power.

Environmental Systems
Climate, material systems, infrastructure, ecology, environmental performance, and relationships between architecture and larger physical networks.

Decolonial Inquiry
Questioning which forms of architectural and mathematical knowledge are treated as universal, and which cultural knowledge systems remain outside formal institutions.
